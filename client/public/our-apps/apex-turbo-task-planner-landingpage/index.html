All your tasks, scratchpad notes, Pomodoro session stats, and streak history are stored strictly within Chrome's local storage API on your machine. Nothing is ever sent to any remote server.
Click the Focus Dock or hit the play button on any task. The Deep Charcoal Focus screen launches with a 25-minute timer, circular SVG dial, and audio chimes. You can also switch to 5-minute short breaks or 15-minute long breaks.
Under Settings, you can enable reminders and pick your preferred alert time (e.g. 10:00 AM). Apex Turbo leverages the Chrome Alarms API to deliver a native desktop notification listing your pending tasks.
Yes! With one click on the Backup button, you can download a complete JSON export of all tasks, notes, and history. You can restore this file at any time on any device.
Apex Turbo is built on Chrome Manifest V3 and runs seamlessly on Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Opera, and any Chromium-based browser.
Press Alt + Shift + T on Windows & Linux, or Option + Shift + T on Mac to instantly launch Apex Turbo from anywhere in your browser without clicking the toolbar icon. You can also customize your preferred shortcut anytime in Chrome's extension shortcuts settings (chrome://extensions/shortcuts).
