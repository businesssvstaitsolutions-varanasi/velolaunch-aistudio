Frequently Asked Questions

No! Apex Tornado includes a dedicated Cache Only preset that exclusively purges temporary HTTP files, media buffers, and script shards while preserving your session cookies and authentication tokens. Additionally, the Protected Websites whitelist guarantees your primary work domains (like Google, GitHub, and Slack) are never touched.

Apex Tornado features intelligent Pause Conditions. If a scheduled tab is currently active (you are looking at it), playing audio/video (YouTube, Spotify, Zoom), or whitelisted, the reload timer automatically pauses and waits until you navigate away before safely completing the reload.

Apex Tornado requests only standard Chromium permissions required for local operation: browsingData (to remove cache and storage), tabs (to reload and monitor tab status), storage (to persist your rules locally), and alarms (for scheduled automations). None of your data is ever transmitted externally.
